# Multi-Format Document Analysis

Current entry point: the [provisional core-suite workflow](../../../docs/core-suite-v0.1.md)
includes both document cases and separates historical measurements from the
corrected evaluation for future supplier runs.

This task family evaluates evidence reconciliation across real office-document
formats. It keeps two conditions separate:

- Track A reasons over one frozen canonical extraction;
- Track B evaluates a complete system using a frozen read-only artifact tool
  surface.

The first development case is
`agentic.documents.rollout-readiness-reconciliation`. Its semantic contract and
verifier are implemented. The `.docx` and `.xlsx` artifacts are authored,
rendered and qualified under explicit alternative-runtime amendments. The
`.pdf` is likewise authored, extracted, rendered and qualified under its
explicit ReportLab amendment. The 15-record canonical Track A representation
is schema-validated, rebuilt from the frozen binaries and qualified without
controlled-answer leakage. The bounded artifact backend and both complete
Track B surfaces are now qualified without model inference. Mini and pinned Pi
share identical backend bytes; Pi exposes only the four custom read operations
with built-ins disabled. Excluded smokes for Mini v0.1 and corrected Pi v0.3
both passed 11/11 semantic checks. Two earlier Pi identities remain preserved
as zero-inference process/extension qualification failures. A tool-free Track A
runner and resumable public-safe controller are offline-qualified. The frozen
15-cell plan observes each of five active-default deployments once under Track
A, Mini v0.1 and Pi v0.3. The separately authorized measured sweep completed
all 15 cells once with no retry: 2/15 semantic successes and 12/15 strict
transports. The supplemental prose audit accepts 7/13 parseable reports and
both deterministic successes.

A separate targeted Qwen 3.8 confirmation was frozen, hash-authorized and
completed. It ran three fresh attempts per unchanged condition in a
position-balanced nine-cell order, reused no historical observation and made
no retry. All 9/9 cells completed; the result is 5/9 semantic successes, 6/9
strict transports and 79/99 essential checks. Mini is 3/3 semantic and strict,
Pi is 2/3 semantic and 0/3 strict, and canonical Track A is 0/3 semantic and
3/3 strict. The separate prose audit accepts 6/9 reports. These are bounded
consistency counts, not a population reliability probability or universal
ranking.

OCR, arbitrary document editing, Word report assembly and spreadsheet repair
are separate future tasks. A result in this family must not be used as evidence
for those capabilities.

The second case, `agentic.documents.supplier-award-reconciliation`, is
qualified through the artifact-API layer and both complete Track B systems.
It changes the
domain and reasoning shape: four suppliers are filtered by policy, signed-final
assurance, cost and capacity, then only eligible suppliers are ranked for
primary and reserve selection. Atlas is the controlled primary and Boreal the
reserve; Cedar is commercially cheapest but assurance-ineligible, and Delta
fails a formula-derived capacity gate. The reference passes 12/12 deterministic
checks. All three source binaries passed structural and visual QA, their
16-record canonical extraction rebuilds byte-for-byte, and the bounded Track B
backend has exact parity with it. Mini v0.2 and Pi v0.4 are separately
hash-qualified against those backend bytes. Pi v0.4 admits output only through
one schema-constrained `finish_report` tool and passed a real 13/13 no-inference
Seatbelt acceptance. The separately frozen excluded smoke completed one Qwen
3.8 attempt under Pi v0.4 followed by one under Mini v0.2 on Ollama 0.40.2,
without retries. Both runtime and strict transports passed; the frozen checks
were 11/12 and 10/12, and both separate prose reviews found an unsupported
signed-draft claim. See the [smoke report](../../../reports/agentic/multi-format-transfer-smoke-v0.3.md).

Future supplier cells in the core suite explicitly use verifier 2.1.0 and
metrics 0.2 through a fresh-run evaluation identity. The old smoke was not
rescored or promoted to measured evidence. Its attempts are consumed; the
first new comparison remains a draft with no selected candidate or live
execution authorization.
